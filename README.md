# ClassicModels Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the ClassicModels sales database using MySQL to identify business insights related to sales, customers, products, revenue, and business performance.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales data and answer important business questions that can help management understand:

- Overall revenue performance
- Top-performing products
- Top customers
- Revenue by country
- Sales representative performance
- Product line performance
- Sales trends over time
- Customer purchasing behavior

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Total Company Revenue

The company generated a total revenue of **$9,604,190.61** based on the available order details.

### 2. Top Products by Revenue

The **1992 Ferrari 360 Spider red** generated the highest revenue among the analyzed products, with total revenue of **$276,839.98**.

Other high-revenue products included:

- 2001 Ferrari Enzo — $190,755.86
- 1952 Alpine Renault 1300 — $190,017.96
- 1980s Black Hawk Helicopter — $144,959.91
- 1968 Ford Mustang — $161,531.48
